About

I am a PhD student in economics at the University of Toronto, working on networks and information: how the pattern of who connects to who shapes what markets know, what they reward, and who gets ahead.

Before Toronto I completed an MA at Columbia, where I wrote a theory of endogenous network formation in information markets under Evan Sadler, and a BA at Chung-Ang University in Seoul, where I mapped the collaboration networks of YouTube creators.

Hockey, and sports analytics in general, is my other interest. The Fabric of the NHL, an interactive map of every teammate relationship in the modern league, is the first public piece of that work.

I also write (as often as I can) about everything and anything else on my Substack, No Fixed Position.
