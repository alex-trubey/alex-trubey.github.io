One map of the modern NHL as a social fabric: roughly 2,400 players and more than 230,000 shared-roster ties, drawn from full career histories. Search any player, trace the connection between two careers, and filter ties by where they formed, from junior leagues to the NHL itself. Built in Python, rendered as a single hand-written page with WebGL.
